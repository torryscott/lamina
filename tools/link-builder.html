Assignment Link Builder

Choose which brain areas your students should be tested on. Unselected areas are skipped for that assignment; leave everything selected to use the full module.

How this works. Unchecking areas adds their codes to the link, like ?off=D1.M3; everything not listed stays visible. The homepage, every atlas, and every quiz honor it. Nothing to configure — share the link. Only views that have been mapped are live; the rest show as in progress.
