Lamina.

Sheep brain neuroanatomy — an interactive atlas and a practicum quiz, across twelve views of a dissected specimen.

lamina — Latin, a plate or layer. Both the laminae of the cortex and the plate of an anatomical atlas.

Instructors: the link builder generates a student link covering exactly the structures you want. Everything here respects it.
